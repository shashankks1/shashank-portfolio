02 — Colour

Blue is the ink, not an accent

Your CV already sets your name and headings in blue. The system keeps that: SKS blue writes the name, the headings and every stroke. Text is a blue-black, like indigo-dyed cotton, so even the body copy belongs to the mark.

06 — Left behind

What made v2 look generated

These are the moves AI site builders reach for by default. None of them had a reason rooted in you.

  • Instrument Serif + Inter + a monospace → your own Baskerville + Inter
  • A poster-size name with an orange full stop → the name at a reading size, in your blue
  • Monospace uppercase labels everywhere → the CV's bold caps
  • “(05)” counters and “Fig. 01” captions → plain numbers where they help
  • Visible grid lines as decoration → a grid you feel, not see
  • A black “Let's work together.” slab → contact in the same voice as the rest
  • Orange from nowhere → the blue of your mark
  • Aphorism cards and a three-column skills list → content decided later, with you
