I design brands and the systems that keep them good: identities, product interfaces, and the rules behind the work.

Since May 2026 I've been Brand & Product Design Lead at Vistofy, a platform that gives small Indian businesses their marketing: festival posts, digital cards, quotations. I own the identity, the product design system and the template line, and I work with a team of designers.

Before that I designed identities and websites for founders: an architecture firm in Jaipur, a craft label from Assam, a skills programme for women entrepreneurs.

A lot of my production now runs through AI tools: image models, scripts, plugin automation. I write the rules, make the picks and check every output; the tools do the volume. Each case study says which part was which.
