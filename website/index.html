How I work

Systems that carry taste

  1. 01

    A rule is taste, written down.

    The festival line's 3×3 grid and variety matrix exist so images made on different days, by different hands, still don't look alike.

    Vistofy Festival Line
  2. 02

    Decide at the smallest size.

    The Vistofy mark was redrawn for a 16-pixel browser tab and a rubber stamp before it was allowed onto a poster.

    Vistofy Identity
  3. 03

    Let the work be the loudest thing.

    Indi Teppich's palette had to pass one test: cover the product photos, and if the colours feel generic, they're working too hard.

    Indi Teppich
  4. 04

    Start where the people are.

    Learnet's app began in six cities: 157 surveys and 22 interviews before the first screen was drawn.

    Learnet Skills
