15 — Case Study / Body Text (65ch)

The Brief

The client needed a mobile learning platform that could deliver vocational training to women in rural and semi-urban areas — in multiple Indian languages, on low-end Android devices, with limited data connectivity.

The design challenge was not just interface — it was literacy, trust, and cultural context across six different cities with vastly different user profiles.

Research approach

We conducted field research across Jaipur, Tiruppur, Ludhiana, Kolkata, Surat, and Delhi — observing workers in garment factories, interviewing supervisors, and running contextual inquiry sessions with the target users.

16 — Case Study / Pull Quote

"The women already knew how to learn — they learned by watching each other. The platform needed to feel like that, not like a classroom."
