Brand Identity

COPO

Logo, logo manual and printed collateral for an e-commerce brand selling handcrafted goods from Assam.

Client COPO (The Copo)
Year 2023
Scope Logo design, logo manual, colour, brand collateral
Live thecopo.in ↗

The Brief

COPO is an e-commerce brand selling handcrafted products from Assam: handloom textiles, handicrafts, gifting items and souvenirs rooted in regional artisanship. They needed a logo and identity that could carry that cultural weight without becoming a heritage cliché.

Scope: logo design, the logo's colour system and printed collateral (banners, letterhead, visiting card, tags, stickers). The website was handled separately by the client.

Exploration

The mark and the wordmark were explored together, then the wordmark was tested across a wide colour range before narrowing to one direction.

Exploration boards: mark and wordmark pairings, and the wordmark across a colour range.

The Mark

A symmetrical mark in deep plum, set above a soft, rounded wordmark, on a dusty pink. It works as a rectangular lockup for print and inside a circle for profile pictures and stickers.

Logo Manual

A 14-page manual so the logo survives other people using it: rectangular and circular versions with clear space, favicons for dark and light backgrounds, print and digital colour values, Noto Sans as the typeface, and the logo applied to stationery, cards, envelopes, tags and social media.

Clear space, circular logo, favicons for dark and light, colour, typeface, stationery, tags.

Collateral

Visiting card, letterhead, product tags and stickers.

The circular logo as the Instagram display picture.

Logo, logo manual and collateral are mine. The website at thecopo.in is the client's own Shopify setup; I recommended a redesign, and the client chose to keep it.
